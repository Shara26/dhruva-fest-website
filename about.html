About Dhruva

Dhruva is the annual cultural fest of our college, organized to celebrate creativity, talent, and cultural diversity among students.

The fest provides a platform for students to showcase their skills in dance, music, drama, and various cultural activities.

Dhruva Fest 2026 will be held from 28 to 30 January with multiple events spread across three exciting days.
